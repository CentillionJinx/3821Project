The final puzzle Candidate 2, kept NP ∩ NP-hard

Bodies overlap. Organs do not.

A blob is a connected polyomino A = (T(A), O(A)): body tiles T(A) and a non-empty set of vital organs O(A) ⊆ T(A). Blobs are pre-placed and may share body tissue freely. The one prohibition is contact between an organ and any tile of another selected blob. Choose a sub-collection B that respects that lock and covers at least k distinct tiles.

Decision problem

Given a grid G, a collection C of pre-placed blobs, and a quota k, is there a witness B ⊆ C such that O(A) ∩ T(A′) = ∅ for every ordered pair of distinct blobs A, A′ ∈ B, and |⋃ T(A)| ≥ k?

The witness is the certificate. The verifier never searches. O(|B|²·|T|)
01
Body overlap is legal

Shared tissue makes a compact board possible, and it lets a blob act as a wire.

02
An organ is a one-way lock

The constraint is local: it depends on two blobs, not on the rest of the board.

03
Coverage is a quota, not an objective

Padding a clause block turns ≥ k into an existential force: miss the block, miss the quota.

The contract

Four checks. One pass. No search.

deterministic 
verify(G, C, B, k):
  assert B ⊆ C                 # 1. membership
  U := ∅
  for A in B: U := U ∪ T(A)  # 2. union, dedup
  for A, D in pairs(B):          # 3. organ lock
      assert O(A) ∩ T(D) = ∅
  return |U| ≥ k               # 4. quota
MembershipO(|B|)
Union and dedupO(|B|·|T|)
Pairwise organ lockO(|B|²·|T|)
QuotaO(1)

Total O(|B|²·|T|) time and O(NM + Σ|T|) space. A yes is checkable without trusting the solver; a no is the hard part.

Why it is hard

3-SAT → Grid-Organ, in four gadgets

Gadget argument complete; layout case analysis still open.
G1 mutex

Truth bank

Two blobs per variable, each organ inside the other’s body. Selecting both breaks the invariant, so a witness picks at most one value.

G2 private

Occurrence blob

One blob per literal occurrence, carrying a private clause block. Nobody else covers it, and k counts it once.

G3 3-way

Clause triangle

The three occurrence blobs of a clause are pairwise organ-locked. At most one is selected, so a clause cannot buy two literals.

G4 force

Quota lift

k equals all private tiles, and each clause block is padded wider than every non-private tile. Coverage ≥ k forces every block.

Forward · a satisfying assignment gives a witness

Select one true literal occurrence per clause. Every private block is covered, so the union clears k. The triangle never contributes two literals and the truth bank never contributes both values, so no organ lock is crossed.

Backward · a witness gives a satisfying assignment

Padding makes coverage ≥ k impossible unless every clause block is covered, so each clause supplies one selected literal. The triangle allows at most one; the truth bank forbids xi and ¬xi together.

Open item: the four gadgets are not yet a full reduction. The missing step is a planar layout with a polynomial grid bound and a proof that no unintended organ-body contact appears. We state it as the next piece of work, not a footnote.
